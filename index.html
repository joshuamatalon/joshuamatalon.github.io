TEMPO — live draft aid, auction and snake

Fantasy-football drafts give you ten seconds to decide whether $77 for a running back is a good deal, with eleven people bidding against you. TEMPO puts one number on every player, built from four projection systems, two seasons of play-by-play, cited research on 250 players and the room's own bidding history, and re-solves your whole roster plan after every sale or pick. Every number opens into the seven steps that made it.

Ran four real drafts in 2026: two salary-cap auctions and two snake drafts. In a 12-team auction it logged 169 of 170 sales live, every price and buyer matching the official results, and the roster graded first of twelve at the room's actual prices.

How I work

1

Spec first

You get a short written spec before anything is built, so we agree on what done looks like while changing it is free.

2

Sample early

The first piece lands within a day: a sample tab, a mapped page, a one-page outline. You approve the shape before I finish the rest.

3

Prove it

Every deliverable is checked against something real: your control totals, the source page, the rulebook, the official results. I say what I could not verify instead of rounding it up.

4

Fixed price

One number, agreed up front, with what is included and what is not written down. Changes are priced before they happen.
