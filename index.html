How I work

1

Spec first

You get a short written spec before anything is built, so we agree on what done looks like while changing it is still free.

2

Sample early

The first piece lands within a day, whether that is a sample tab, a mapped page or a one-page outline. You approve the shape before I finish the rest.

3

Prove it

Every deliverable is checked against something real, like your control totals, the source page, the rulebook or the official results. Where I could not verify something, I say so instead of rounding it up.

4

Fixed price

One number, agreed up front, with what is included and what is not written down. Changes are priced before they happen.

Installable web apps that work offline and need no account. I build them to replace a paper sheet, not to impress an app store.

What I will not do. Nothing automated submits, signs or sends on your behalf without a person checking it. If you could buy it cheaper than I can build it, I say so. After delivery you get documentation and a trained owner, and I stay reachable.
